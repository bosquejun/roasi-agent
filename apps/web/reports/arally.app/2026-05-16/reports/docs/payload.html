Why Arally

Built for teachers who don't want a black box.

Most AI tools hand teachers a finished output and call it done. Arally hands you the building blocks.

We started Arally because the best teaching materials don't come out of a template — they come out of a teacher who knows their class, their pacing, and what they tried last week that didn't quite land.

So instead of one big “generate” button, Arally is a composable canvas: lesson, worksheet, quiz, rubric, slides — pulled together however your week needs them, with controls for tone, difficulty, length, and reading level along the way.

The work stays yours. The shortcuts are ours.

— The Arally team

Our Story

Built by someone who gets it.

We saw teachers spending hours adapting materials that didnt fit their students. Pre-made templates, generic outputs, one-size-fits-all AI that asked all the wrong questions.

As computer education graduates, we knew there had to be a better way — so we built Arally to hand that time back to educators.

The work should fit your class. Not the other way around.
