Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arally.

Arally provides a flexible canvas to compose teaching workflows — shaped around your subject, your students, and your style.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Account information (such as name and email)
  • Content you create within Arally (e.g. teaching materials, workflows, notes)
  • Any feedback or support requests you send to us

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use Arally, we may collect:

  • Device and browser information
  • Usage data (such as interactions with features)
  • Log data (including IP address and timestamps)

2.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain session state
  • Improve user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Service
  • Enable creation and storage of your content
  • Improve platform functionality and user experience
  • Debug, secure, and maintain system performance
  • Respond to support requests

4. Content You Create

Content you create in Arally belongs to you.

We store and process your content only to:

  • Make the Service function
  • Enable syncing across devices
  • Provide optional assisted features

We do not claim ownership of your content.

5. Assisted Features and External Processing

Some features in Arally may process your inputs through external service providers to generate or refine content.

By using these features, you acknowledge that:

  • Your inputs may be transmitted to third-party systems for processing
  • These providers may vary depending on availability and performance
  • We may update or change providers over time

We encourage you not to submit sensitive personal data or confidential information unless necessary.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share limited information with:

  • Infrastructure providers (hosting, storage, analytics)
  • External service providers required to operate the platform
  • Legal authorities if required by law

All third parties are expected to handle data securely and only for service operation purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service
  • Maintain account functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

10. Children’s Privacy

Arally is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction).

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your own, depending on where our infrastructure and service providers are located.

We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact: privacy@arally.app
